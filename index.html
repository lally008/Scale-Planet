The price of a weighing scale in Pakistan depends on the type, weighing capacity, platform size, accuracy, indicator, load cells, material quality, and application. Small commercial digital scales are generally suitable for shops and counters, while heavy-duty platform scales, industrial scales, animal scales, crane scales, laboratory balances, and other specialized weighing systems are designed for different requirements.

At Scale Planet, we provide weighing solutions for shops, businesses, warehouses, factories, industries, laboratories, agriculture, livestock, and commercial applications. Available capacities can range from small digital scales to high-capacity industrial weighing systems.

For an accurate price, it is best to specify the required capacity, platform size, scale type, and application. Scale Planet can help you select a weighing scale according to your requirements and budget.

The right weighing scale depends on what you need to weigh and how frequently the scale will be used. For retail shops and counters, a digital counter weighing scale may be suitable for everyday products. For warehouses, factories, and heavier goods, a platform weighing scale is generally more appropriate.

For specialized applications, businesses may require price computing scales, platform scales, label or barcode scales, crane scales, animal scales, counting scales, or laboratory balances.

When selecting a commercial weighing scale, consider:

  • Maximum weighing capacity
  • Required accuracy and readability
  • Platform or pan size
  • Frequency of use
  • Indoor or industrial environment
  • Digital indicator and display
  • Tare and zero functions
  • Power/battery requirements
  • Connectivity requirements, if applicable

Scale Planet can help you choose a weighing scale based on your product, capacity, and business application rather than simply choosing a scale by price.

A digital weighing scale uses electronic sensors, commonly load cells, to measure weight and display the result electronically. A mechanical weighing scale uses mechanical components such as springs, levers, or other mechanical mechanisms to indicate weight.

Digital scales are widely used because they can provide easy-to-read digital measurements and may include features such as tare, zero, counting, price calculation, battery operation, data interfaces, or other electronic functions, depending on the model.

Mechanical scales can still be useful for certain applications where a simple non-electronic weighing system is preferred.

The best choice depends on your application, required accuracy, operating environment, capacity, and available features. Scale Planet offers different weighing solutions for commercial, industrial, laboratory, agricultural, livestock, and other applications.

Choosing the correct weighing capacity is important for accurate and reliable weighing. First, determine the maximum weight you normally need to measure, then select a scale with an appropriate capacity and suitable readability for your application.

For example:

  • 30KG scale – suitable for many small retail, commercial, kitchen, parcel, and general weighing applications.
  • 100KG scale – useful for heavier products and commercial applications.
  • 300KG scale – commonly considered for larger goods and heavier commercial weighing.
  • 500KG scale – suitable for many industrial and warehouse applications.
  • 1 Ton weighing scale – designed for substantially heavier industrial and material-handling requirements.

Capacity alone is not enough. You should also consider platform size, load-cell capacity, accuracy, readability, installation conditions, and the type of material being weighed.

If you are unsure whether you need a 300KG, 500KG, 1 Ton, or another capacity, contact Scale Planet with your product type, approximate maximum weight, and required platform size. We can help identify a suitable weighing solution.

Regular calibration helps maintain confidence in weighing results. The appropriate calibration interval depends on the type of scale, frequency of use, operating environment, required accuracy, and applicable quality or regulatory requirements.

Pakistan National Accreditation Council (PNAC) guidance states that weighing machines should be traceably calibrated and should be calibrated after servicing or at least once per year, with calibration documented by a calibration certificate. PNAC also recommends regular checks between calibrations using suitable control weights.

A scale should also be checked if you notice:

  • Unstable or inconsistent readings
  • A noticeable difference from known test weights
  • The scale has been moved or installed in a new location
  • The scale has been overloaded or physically damaged
  • Load cells, indicator, or other major components have been serviced or replaced

For commercial weighing equipment, applicable verification and regulatory requirements should also be considered. In Punjab, the Directorate General of Industries, Prices, Weights & Measures is responsible for inspection and verification of commercial weights and measures.

For industrial, warehouse, and factory applications, the correct platform weighing scale depends on the maximum load, platform dimensions, weighing environment, required accuracy, installation method, and type of material being weighed.

Heavy-duty platform scales are commonly used for weighing items such as bags, boxes, drums, pallets, raw materials, finished goods, and other industrial products. Depending on the application, a weighing system may include a steel platform, load cells, digital indicator, junction box, ramps, and other accessories.

Before purchasing an industrial weighing scale, consider:

  • Maximum capacity
  • Platform size
  • Load-cell specification
  • Indicator type
  • Required readability
  • Floor or pit installation
  • Ramp requirements
  • Indoor or outdoor environment
  • Moisture, dust, and operating conditions
  • Required calibration or verification
