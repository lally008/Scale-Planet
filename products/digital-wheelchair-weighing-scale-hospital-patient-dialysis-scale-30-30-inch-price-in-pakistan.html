Where Can This Scale Be Used?

The Digital Wheelchair Weighing Scale is specially designed for weighing patients who cannot stand comfortably on a regular weighing scale. It is ideal for:

- Hospitals: Regular patient weight monitoring.
- Dialysis Centres: Checking patient weight before and after dialysis sessions.
- Medical Clinics: Weighing patients with limited mobility.
- Rehabilitation Centres: Monitoring patients during recovery.
- Nursing Homes: Weighing elderly and wheelchair-dependent patients.
- Healthcare Facilities: Convenient patient weight measurement.

Why Should You Choose This Wheelchair Weighing Scale?

The Digital Wheelchair Weighing Scale provides a convenient and practical weighing solution for patients with mobility difficulties.

Its spacious 30 × 30 inch platform allows wheelchair access through the entry ramp. The two side safety handrails provide support for patients to hold while using the scale.
