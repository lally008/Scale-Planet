20
Years in the field

We've been solving measurement problems since 2005.

We, M/S Scale Planet, have been working in the field of digital weighing scales for over two decades — providing the best services and quality products to ensure the efficiency of your weighing needs.

We provide all types of platform scales of any size and capacity. Because we have our own in-house fabrication workshop, we can build your platform scale exactly to your demand, design, and capacity — not just pick from a catalogue.

Our philosophy is simple: if it needs to be measured, it needs to be measured right. Every product we sell, every service we offer, is built around that one idea.
